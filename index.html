SMALL STEPS MATTER

You Are Not Alone

Practical ways to handle everyday student pressure.

📖

Plan Your Studies

Break big assignments into small tasks. Set realistic goals and ask teachers for help.

⏳

Manage Your Time

Choose three priorities each day. Schedule breaks and get enough sleep.

💙

Care for Your Mind

Talk to someone you trust, take breaks and seek support when stress feels overwhelming.

💵

Manage Money

Track spending, compare book prices and ask your college about scholarships.

📵

Set Message Boundaries

Mute unnecessary notifications and check messages at specific times.

🌱

Believe in Yourself

Celebrate progress. Your worth is bigger than a test score.
